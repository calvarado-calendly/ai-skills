# Nomination Packet

Use this structure for a full peer review or 360-feedback nomination packet. Adapt headings lightly when the user's review system uses different terminology.

## Inputs Used

State the review subject, review period, desired nominee count, requester-supplied direct-report context, and any org-chart or roster source checked.

If a required input was missing and the user chose to proceed anyway, say that plainly.

## Recommended Nominees

Rank candidates in order of likely feedback value.

For each candidate include:

- `Recommendation`: recommend, alternate, or do not recommend.
- `Confidence`: high, medium, or low.
- `Relationship to subject`: peer, cross-functional partner, manager-adjacent collaborator, likely direct report, likely indirect report, unknown, or other accurate label.
- `Why this person`: concise summary of the distinct feedback perspective they can provide.
- `Evidence`: dated bullets with source, artifact or thread, interaction type, and what it shows.
- `Feedback dimensions`: the behaviors or impact areas this person can likely speak to.
- `Caveats`: reporting-line uncertainty, limited source coverage, narrow interaction scope, private-source sensitivity, or outdated evidence.

Use source links when available. Avoid long quotes from private channels or DMs unless the user explicitly asks for them.

## Alternates

Include alternates when the user requested them or when the evidence suggests useful backup choices.

For each alternate, explain why they were not in the top set, such as narrower interaction scope, lower evidence confidence, duplicate perspective, or reporting-line concern.

## Excluded Or Downgraded Candidates

List notable people who might appear relevant but should not be prioritized.

Common reasons:

- Direct report or likely indirect report.
- Mostly passive meeting overlap.
- Only automated, administrative, or broadcast interaction.
- Meaningful interaction is outside the review period.
- Perspective is already covered by stronger nominees.
- Evidence is too thin to support a substantive review.

## Source Coverage And Gaps

Provide a concise source table or bullets with:

- Source searched.
- Query or artifact pattern used.
- Date boundary.
- Coverage status: searched, incomplete, not available, or not searched.
- Important limitations.

Call out any pagination, access, or connector limits that affected confidence.

## Suggested Request Strategy

If useful, recommend how to request feedback:

- Who should be in the first wave.
- Who can be used as backups.
- Which feedback angle to ask each person to emphasize.
- Any sensitive handling needed because of private-source evidence or reporting-line ambiguity.
